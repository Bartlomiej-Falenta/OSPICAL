# OSPICAL
Open Source Photonic Integrated Circuit Assembly Line
as well as: Optomod (component discussed more [here](https://github.com/Bartlomiej-Falenta/OSPICAL/tree/main/optomodComponent)), but tl;dr it's meant to act like transistor for beams of light.

While the OSPICAL itself aims to establish manufacturing photonics (and to lesser degree electronics) as free and open source as possible. It's going to achieve it by utilizing what is already developed by [HackerFab](https://docs.hackerfab.org/home), [MakerFoundry](https://hackaday.io/project/25260-makerfoundry), or even some designs by [TheThoughtEmporium](https://github.com/thethoughtemporium) + a lot of solutions and custom designs based around repurposing and modifying commonly available items or devices (microscope and camera lenses, 3D printers, video projectors, ovens, vacuum pumps, PC parts, car parts, etc.), also a lot of clever software (for e.g. generating mask images, controlling sputtering systems or nanosteppers). Generally, OSPICAL is also focused on documentation and tutorials, not on just technologies themselves.

We still need to find out what is and isn't possible or accessible, but can make it work, one way or another.
